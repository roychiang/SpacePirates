About the Game

Space Pirates is an open-source browser-based 3D space shooter originally created as a demo for the Babylon.js 5.0 release. It features stunning WebGL graphics, real-time multiplayer combat, and global leaderboards — all running natively in your web browser without any download or installation.

Choose to play solo against AI-controlled enemy ships, or team up with friends in real-time co-op mode using peer-to-peer networking. Track your progress on global leaderboards and compete for the highest scores.

Key Features

🚀 3D Space Combat

Stunning WebGL-powered 3D graphics with post-processing effects, particle systems, and immersive space environments rendered by Babylon.js.

👥 Real-time Multiplayer

Team up with friends in co-op mode via peer-to-peer connections. No lag, no waiting — direct browser-to-browser gameplay.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Space Pirates?

Space Pirates is a free, browser-based multiplayer 3D space shooter built with Babylon.js and WebGL. You can play solo against AI enemies or team up with friends in real-time co-op — no download required.

Is Space Pirates free to play?

Yes, Space Pirates is completely free to play. Just open your web browser and go to www.spacepirates.app to start playing instantly.

How do I play multiplayer?

Space Pirates supports real-time co-op multiplayer via peer-to-peer connections. You can team up with up to 8 players directly in your browser without any additional software.

What are the controls?

Use WASD or Arrow Keys to move your ship, the mouse to aim and fire, and Spacebar for boost. Full gamepad/controller support is also available.

What technology is it built with?

Space Pirates is built with Babylon.js 5.x for 3D WebGL rendering, TypeScript, Colyseus for real-time multiplayer game state sync, and PeerJS for peer-to-peer co-op connections.

Does Space Pirates have leaderboards?

Yes, Space Pirates features global leaderboards that track your kills, wins, and overall score. Compete with other players worldwide for the top spots.
